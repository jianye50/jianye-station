What do you see every time you open a new tab in your browser? Full screen push news, pop-up ads, and a variety of content recommendations. Many start pages are constantly stacked with functions, turning the new tab page into an information flow plaza. We only want to search quickly, but we are wrapped up in all kinds of information and our attention is constantly chopped up.

![屏幕截图_17-10-2026_13579_jianye.pages.dev](屏幕截图_17-10-2026_13579_jianye.pages.dev.jpg)

So I started to make **Jianye's Homepage**, a simple, clean, and efficient browser new tab page, returning to what the browser start page should be: pure, efficient, and non-intrusive.

## 🧩 Design philosophy: do subtraction, not addition
Many homepage products on the market are keen on adding functions, components, and information flows. The core idea of ​​this homepage is to eliminate redundancy and retain what is just needed.

- ❌ No pop-ups, no redundant recommendations, and no fancy ads
- ✅ Restrain a clean layout and remove all distracting elements
- ✅ Completely free to use, no membership fees, no hidden ads

When you open a new tab, the page will not forcefully stuff you with any content, leaving the choice back to the user.

## ⚡ Core Features
### Minimalist design, zero distractions
The entire page is visually restrained and left blank, with no fancy animations or decorations. When you open the browser, your eyes will not be robbed by cluttered elements, and you can focus on the search and browsing tasks you really want to complete.

### Extremely fast loading, instant start experience
Abandon bloated resources and do not load a large number of pictures and scripts. Regardless of whether the network is good or bad, the tab will open instantly without spinning around waiting for rendering. Even low-end devices can run smoothly.

### Central search box, available out of the box
The search box is placed directly in the center of the page, and the cursor is automatically focused when opening a tab. Just enter keywords to search directly, eliminating the need for multiple jumps and going directly to the search in one step, making searching simple.

### Simple and practical, returning to the essence of search
Keep basic practical elements such as time display and cut off flashy components. Don’t pile up gadgets, don’t pile up information flows, and return to the original positioning of the browser homepage: the entrance to the Internet, not the place for content consumption.
