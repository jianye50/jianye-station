A coffee shop with lights on late at night. You don’t need to download the client, just open the browser and you can immerse yourself in this virtual street corner world.

✨Project Introduction

This is an interactive 3D web scene based on Three.js (WebGL) pure front-end implementation. All models are generated programmatically, without importing external GLB model files. It relies on basic geometry, canvas dynamic mapping, and material lighting to build a complete street and coffee shop environment.

After opening the link, you can drag the mouse to rotate the perspective, use the scroll wheel to zoom in and out, and right-click to pan. You can freely shuttle between street view, in-store, bird's-eye view, and low-angle camera positions to experience the complete day and night changes of a 24-hour corner coffee shop from early morning, noon, dusk, and late at night.

The entire scene does not rely on a game engine. It only needs an ordinary browser to run directly. You can enter this slow-paced virtual world by opening the web page.

☕What’s in the scene?

The scene is divided into the main body of a two-story coffee shop, with a flower shop next door, city buildings in the distance, and a complete street environment, full of details:

Coffee shop interior: coffee machine, grinder, cake display cabinet, coffee bean bag shelves, tables, chairs and sofas, floor lamps, hanging pictures and blackboard menus, potted green plants, second-floor leisure sofa reading area, chandelier light strips that automatically switch on and off day and night.

External street environment: sidewalks, asphalt roads, zebra crossings, street lights, street trees, bicycles, self-driving cars, traffic lights, vending machines, mailboxes, fire hydrants, street outdoor coffee tables and chairs. There is a small flower shop next to it, and in the distance there are city buildings and thousands of lights.

Cute little easter eggs: the kitten curled up on the street corner, the smoke curling up from the roof chimney, and the street decorative string lights.

🎛Core interactive functions

The floating control panel on the left can adjust the status of the entire world in real time:

Time control system Drag the time slider to jump freely to 24 hours a day; turn on "Time Flow" and the world will automatically pass time. The color of the sky, the position of the moon and sun, stars, and building lights will change in real time with time. Late at night, all the lights in the store slowly light up, and the stars appear in the sky; in the morning, the sun rises, and the lights slowly go out.

Four weather simulations Sunny☀️/Cloudy☁️/Raining🌧️/Snowing❄️

Rain: raindrop particles, reflection of water on the road, ambient rain sound;

Snow: Snow is falling all over the sky, and snow accumulates automatically on the ground and roof;

It also supports manual adjustment of cloud cover and precipitation intensity. You can also turn on the ambient sound effects and listen to wind and rain white noise.

Switch between multiple perspectives with one click

Street view: Standing across the road and looking at the entire coffee shop building;

Inside the store: Go directly inside the house and browse the furnishings inside the coffee shop;

Overlooking: a high-altitude God's perspective, overlooking the entire street;

Low angle: feel the street atmosphere close to the ground.

Auto Tour: When turned on, the camera will automatically roam around the entire scene, suitable for viewing as a dynamic background.

🛠Key points of technical implementation

Rendering library: Three.js r128, WebGL rendering, OrbitControls to implement camera drag interaction

Post-effects: Bloom, FXAA anti-aliasing, Gamma correction, soft glowing effect at night

Resource features: All models are generated by program, basic geometry such as Box, Cylinder, Sphere, etc.; Canvas2D draws dynamic textures (floors, brick walls, signs, menus, moon textures), no external texture files, reducing network resource requests.

Dynamic system: day and night illumination interpolation calculation, sky color gradient, sun, moon and star position calculation, particle system to implement rain and snow, car driving logic, traffic light status cycle, light brightness dynamically changes with the environment.

Audio: The Web Audio API generates wind and rain ambient white noise without the need for external audio files.

UI: Native CSS realizes the frosted glass control panel, and has been adapted to the mobile terminal. The experience can also be accessed on mobile phones.

💡Creative ideas

I have always liked the atmosphere of city corner coffee shops: street lights turn on at dusk, warm yellow light shines from the glass windows, and pedestrians and traffic flow slowly outside. So I thought about moving this atmosphere to the browser.

Instead of using a ready-made three-dimensional model, we chose to build it all with code. It was not only a comprehensive exercise for Three.js, but also a small scene that "slowed down". You can turn the time and watch the street be filled with sunshine on a sunny afternoon, or you can switch to snow and watch the shop brightly lit at night when the snow falls, with ambient sound, making it a relaxing digital corner.
